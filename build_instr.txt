Requirements: JDK 25 and Apache Maven.

- mvn package -DskipTests
    builds target/monyrama.jar with its dependencies in target/lib
    (run with: java -jar target/monyrama.jar)
- mac/build-dmg.sh
    builds the macOS installer dist/Monyrama-<version>-<arch>.dmg with jpackage
- linux/monyrama and start.bat are launchers to put next to monyrama.jar and lib/

The app version is <version> in pom.xml.
UI texts are edited in i18n/*.properties (UTF-8), no conversion step is needed.

Known failing tests (unrelated to the build): I18nPropertiesTest and IconsUsageTest use
hard-coded D:/ paths, PrivateBankImportReaderTest and MonoBankImportReaderTest have stale fixtures.
